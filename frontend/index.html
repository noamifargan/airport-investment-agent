Find the US airports where new capacity pays off.

Ask about passenger growth, congestion, route mix or unmet demand. Every answer is built from public BTS, DOT and FAA data, and shows where its numbers came from.

Starting points

  • Estimates the passengers and flights turned away, and the constraint behind them.

Follow-up questions keep the context, so you can ask “why?” or “how does that compare to Boston?”.
