## How to answer

- Lead with the top three airports and their scores (0–100), in rank order.
- For each, give its main strength and main weakness from the ranking.
- Say how many smaller airports were left out by the passenger cutoff.
- Key uncertainty for terminal expansion: the score measures demand pressure (full planes, growth, fares), not the terminal itself.
- Key uncertainty for congestion: delay, taxi and busiest-hour figures cover only the latest month and only the larger US airlines.
