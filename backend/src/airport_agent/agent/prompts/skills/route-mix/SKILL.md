## How to answer

- Lead with the long-haul share of all departures and the distance cutoff used.
- Then give the long-haul share among passenger flights and among cargo flights, and say whether cargo drives the result.
- Name the top long-haul destinations.
- The key uncertainty is that "long-haul" has no official definition; quote the share at one other cutoff.
